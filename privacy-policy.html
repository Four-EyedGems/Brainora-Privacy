Privacy Policy

Brainora — Offline Brain & Puzzle Games
Effective date: May 13, 2026 · Last updated: May 13, 2026

Brainora is offline-first. Your scores, settings, and progress stay on your device and are never sent to us. The only third party that receives data is Google AdMob, which shows a small banner advertisement to support the app. You can review and change your advertising preferences from the in-app Settings screen.

1. Information we do not collect

Brainora itself does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials.
  • Your physical location, GPS coordinates, or precise device location.
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • Your game scores, level progress, achievements, or any other in-app activity.

The following data is stored only on your device using on-device storage (MMKV) and never leaves your phone:

  • Game progress, level completion, stars, and high scores
  • Game settings and preferences (theme, haptics, sound, reduce motion)
  • Daily challenge and workout completion history
  • Per-game statistics and achievements

2. Information collected by Google AdMob

Brainora shows a single banner advertisement using Google AdMob to keep the app free. To serve relevant ads and measure performance, AdMob automatically collects certain technical information:

  • Advertising identifier (AAID on Android, IDFA on iOS) — a resettable, anonymous ID unique to your device. You can reset or limit this from your device's privacy settings at any time.
  • IP address — used to determine approximate country/region so ads can be localized, and for fraud prevention.
  • Device information — device model, OS version, language, network type, and screen dimensions.
  • Ad interaction data — whether an ad was shown, viewed, or tapped.

Data collection by AdMob is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive your individual advertising data; we only see aggregated performance reports in the AdMob console.

3. Your ad consent choices

On first launch the app shows Google's consent form if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a region with similar privacy regulation (such as California under the CCPA/CPRA). You can choose between personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or (where offered) opt out of advertising data use for personalization.

Changing your choice later

Open the app, tap Settings, and select Ad Preferences. This opens Google's consent management form, where you can change your selection at any time. The change takes effect on the next ad request.

If the Ad Preferences row is not visible in Settings, it means your region does not require a consent selection under current regulations.

4. Internet access

Game logic in Brainora is fully offline. Internet is required only for serving ads; if your device is offline, the banner area will simply be empty and every game continues to work normally. Game data, scores, and progress are never transmitted over the network.

5. Third-party services

Brainora integrates with one third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — banner advertising. See Google's privacy policy and AdMob program policies.

Brainora does not use:

  • Third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
  • Crash reporting tools
  • Social media SDKs
  • Attribution / tracking SDKs

6. Advertising

Brainora shows a single banner advertisement at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob. The banner is hidden during active gameplay so it never crowds the game grid. We do not sell your data; AdMob's collection is governed by Google's privacy policy linked above.

7. In-app purchases

Brainora contains no in-app purchases. All content is available for free.

8. Children's privacy

Brainora is intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe a child has used the app and you wish to request deletion of any advertising data Google AdMob may have collected, contact us at the email below and we will help you initiate that request with Google.

9. Permissions

Brainora declares the following permission:

  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (Android 13+) — read the device's advertising identifier so AdMob can serve ads. You can opt out by going to Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

On iOS, Brainora uses the standard App Tracking Transparency framework. You will be asked once whether the app may use the IDFA. Choosing "Ask App Not to Track" disables personalized ads but keeps non-personalized advertising.

Brainora does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, location, files, or any other sensitive device features.

10. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you reside in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or California, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information that has been collected about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time (via the in-app Ad Preferences row in Settings).
  • Object to processing or request restriction.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Because Brainora itself does not collect personal data, requests regarding advertising data should be directed to Google via Google's privacy request form. We will assist with questions; contact details below.

11. Data deletion

Since all on-device data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Using the "Reset All Stats" option in the app's Settings screen.
  • Clearing the app's data through your device's system settings.
  • Uninstalling the app (all local data is removed).

Advertising data held by Google can be deleted via the Google privacy request form linked in section 10.

12. Data retention

  • On-device data (scores, settings, progress) remains until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • AdMob retains advertising data according to Google's retention policy, which you can review in the Google privacy policy linked above.

13. Security

All network communication from the app (limited to ad-serving by Google AdMob) is encrypted with HTTPS. On-device storage is sandboxed by the operating system. We do not transmit your game progress, scores, or any other in-app activity to any server.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be highlighted in the app's release notes on the Play Store and App Store.
